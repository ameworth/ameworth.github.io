# Graph Theory Answers
IN PROGRESS. 

This document contains worked answers and notes corresponding to the lecture notes of
MATH0029: Graph Theory and Combinatorics, as taught by Dr. António Girão in 2025,
written by a student taking the class. A lot of the answers given will focus more on get-
ting the ‘spirit’ of the question correct, rather than stressing technical details of the proofs.
I’ve tried my best to check them all, but please note that these answers are not officially
endorsed, so expect about ⌈n/2⌉ to be actually correct. Please email me with any mistakes
you find.
